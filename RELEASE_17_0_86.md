# 17.0.86 — Operational evidence and reviewed workflows

Implemented on `testing` under the instruction, “Stop the running tests and implement those things.” The full gate `2026-10-08T23-23-11` was stopped and recorded as cancelled/incomplete. This instruction authorizes this implementation batch before outstanding certification. `main` and `mobile` are outside this batch.

1. Manager Brief/readiness now uses configured food-safety checks, temperature results, overdue intervals and corrective-action sign-offs; an authorized backup summary; setup gaps; AI ordering and Python findings. Loading, denied, cached, stale or bounded evidence cannot establish healthy readiness. Known critical findings remain visible when coverage is incomplete.
2. Operational History loads explicit pages for prep, waste, maintenance, readiness/86 events, receiving and classified alerts. Each source shows scanned/returned counts, a 180-day window, cursor, undated records and the 2,000-document scan limit. The analysis has a separate 500-event limit. Workspace and source permissions are checked on the server. Responses omit private diagnostics and unrelated nested records. Legacy records remain supported without pretending their dates are known.
3. The restaurant graph connects approved batch/menu dependencies, ingredient and vendor evidence, menu prices, costs, allergen review provenance, stock/86 risk and substitutions. Conflicting menu prices and ambiguous names remain unresolved; labels without complete review evidence remain unverified. Substitutions require review.
4. Inventory → Vendors includes reviewed catalog evidence: SKU, package, purchase unit, optional price and public source URL. Approval/update/revocation are permission-checked, audited and conflict-guarded. Product research can use exact SKU catalog evidence, marks old evidence stale and still requires invoice approval. Catalog approval never changes stock, costs or orders. A real Firestore test proves PO → partial receiving → invoice approval → stock/unit cost → recipe yield cost → menu cost/margin → 86 risk → ordering suggestions, including exactly-once retry behavior.
5. Item-sales review supports bulk recipes through an explicitly reviewed servings-per-usable-batch conversion bound to the current recipe yield. Smart Prep converts sold servings to production units, handles reviewed current prep units, omits ungrounded waste adjustments, and qualifies recommendations with open-order, historical and unit-consistent cost evidence. Unreceived orders never become stock. Unsupported conversion/prep units and incomplete prep coverage block a numeric recommendation.
6. Attendance review has explicitly approved workspace timezone, grace and open-break thresholds. It matches published overnight shifts to verified punch/roster identities, flags late/missing/early/open-break records for review, and treats DST ambiguity and incomplete sources as unresolved. It does not change punches or pay. Evidence-linked HR checklists retain their source causes; follow-up counts real completion and later matching records without claiming effectiveness from partial history.
7. Customer Help covers readiness evidence, paged history, catalog review, bulk sales conversions, graph impact, attendance, training follow-up and demand-aware schedule review, with existing authorized navigation links.

Targeted business/API, React, real Firestore emulator, source identity, build and desktop/mobile browser checks are recorded in `docs/feature-status.json` and local `test-results/operational-17-0-86*` artifacts. New browser specs are registered in the release universe and testing CI. The real business transaction test is included in the targeted release preflight.

This closes the seven concrete implementation gaps in the accessible audit. The original ChatGPT scheduled-task daily updates and weekly competitor-review conversations are still unavailable, so this is not a claim that every recommendation from those original reports has been reconciled. Provider-specific POS/payments/payroll/GL/reservations, hardware and enterprise procurement integrations remain deferred under the existing plan. These targeted checks do not establish full release or physical-device certification.
